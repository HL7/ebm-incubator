### What is the Evidence Based Medicine on FHIR Incubator Implementation Guide?

The Evidence Based Medicine on FHIR (EBMonFHIR) project is a comprehensive, systematic effort to expand the HL7 FHIR standard from the domain of health data exchange to include the domain of science data exchange. The EBMonFHIR project is relevant for biomedical research, especially clinical research, and clinical decision support.

The EBMonFHIR project introduced multiple resources into the FHIR specification - namely Evidence, EvidenceVariable, and ArtifactAssessment.

The EBMonFHIR project also introduce a PublicationRecord Resource to provide a catalog entry for bibliographic records (like MEDLINE and Embase) and to provide a FHIR structure for cataloguing any knowlege artifact.

As the PublicationRecord Resource was not sufficiently tested for reaching Normative status with FHIR R6, it was moved to the EBMonFHIR Incubator Implementation Guide for further development and testing.
